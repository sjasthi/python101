Range Function

range() describes a sequence of integers. The start is included; the stop is always excluded. Use list(range(...)) to see all the numbers.

range(stop) starts at 0. range(start, stop) steps by 1. A negative step counts down. If the direction cannot reach the stop, the range is empty. range stores a compact description, not a list of every number.
For Loops

A for loop gets the next value automatically; do not manually increase its variable to control the range. An empty sequence runs the body zero times. Use while when repetition depends on a condition rather than visiting each item.
